LSM303AGR

The LSM303AGR is a 3-axis accelerometer and 3-axis magnetometer by STMicroelectronics. This driver supports it over I2C through the uorb interface. The character driver CONFIG_SENSORS_LSM303AGR is a separate, legacy driver.

Application Programming Interface

#include <nuttx/sensors/lsm303agr.h>

int lsm303agr_register_uorb(FAR struct i2c_master_s *i2c, int devno);

The function registers /dev/uorb/sensor_accel<n> and /dev/uorb/sensor_mag<n>, n being devno. The I2C addresses of the LSM303AGR are fixed (0x19 and 0x1e). Both sensors are polled by one kernel thread.

The accelerometer runs in high-resolution (12-bit) mode. Its data rate is the slowest of 1, 10, 25, 50, 100, 200, 400 and 1344 Hz that is at least the requested rate. The temperature field carries the chip temperature sensor.

The magnetometer runs in continuous high-resolution mode with temperature compensation, at 10, 20, 50 or 100 Hz selected the same way. Its temperature field is zero.

The magnetometer is similar to the LIS2MDL, but not compatible: it has no temperature output, for example. Common code of the two drivers could be shared in the future.

SNIOC_SETFULLSCALE

Sets the accelerometer full scale in g: 2 (default), 4, 8 or 16. The magnetometer has a fixed full scale of +/-50 gauss.

SNIOC_WHO_AM_I

Reads the WHO_AM_I register: 0x33 for the accelerometer and 0x40 for the magnetometer.
